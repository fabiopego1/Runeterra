Legends of Runeterra card art (Riot Games), used in the hover cards of the Origin, Source of Power,
Path and Temperament chapters. Files are named `<kind>-<id>.webp`: bg (Origin), ps (Source of Power),
ar (Path) and pe (Temperament), with the ids from js/data-tables.js. 640px wide, cropped from the
official Data Dragon card art (dd.b.pvp.net).
